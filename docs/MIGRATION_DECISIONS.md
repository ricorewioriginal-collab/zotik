# Migration Decisions – 2026-09-26

## Lyra / Mira – RESOLVED
**Decision: keep both.** This is the least destructive and strongest story solution.

### Mira
Mira already existed in the legacy Build 01 village roster, while the new slice developed her into a strong local Lunaris character. She becomes the player's early emotional anchor and local mystery contact. She knows stories/signs around the Moon Forest and withholds part of her knowledge. She does not become the permanent researcher-party character.

### Lyra
Lyra remains the permanent main character defined by the legacy Story/Characters master: researcher/mage/ranged/healing role, family link to Weltenkern research, and long-form relationship/conflict/reconciliation arc with Zotik. Her entrance is moved to the climax/post-climax bridge of the focused vertical slice.

### Why this route
Merging Mira into Lyra would destroy either Mira's grounded Lunaris role or Lyra's established research/family arc and would invalidate several legacy documents. Keeping both preserves prior canon while making the new slice more focused.

## Legacy Build 01
The old Build 01 remains historical input, not executable authority. Timeline/Break, village attack and other broader content are preserved but may be deferred or reintroduced only after M00 identifies what actually exists and the focused slice is stable.

## Existing feature specs
Housing, marketplace, online events, legal/safety, creator, gift codes, emotes, riddles, performance and MCP documents remain retained requirements. They are not deleted merely because they are outside Phase 1.
