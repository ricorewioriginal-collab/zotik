# Character Production Spec
Zotik: orange anthropomorphic fantasy creature, pointed expressive ears, green eyes, light muzzle/chest, large bushy rigged tail, modular adventurer clothing. Tail and ears are emotional animation channels. Facial blend shapes support dialogue. No expensive full-body fur simulation.

Mira: human Lunaris resident, warm/determined, moon-linked local knowledge; early confidante, not Lyra replacement.
Lyra: human researcher/mage/ranged/healing permanent party member; Weltenkern research family connection and long-form emotional arc remain canon.
Professorium: older human eccentric inventor; comic energy with serious Weltenkern history.
