# Lunaris Content Bible – Consolidated
Core dialogue/content from the new strategy is authoritative for the Phase-1 flow. Mira is the early Lunaris confidante; Toren gates the forest behind training; Boro provides merchant/comic texture; Elwen carries old-rift lore; Finn supports exploration; Sari owns the lost-delivery side quest. Orun recognizes Zotik as a Weltenhörer/Resonanzträger. The Weltenklinge is recovered after the Orun encounter and reacts to Zotik. Professorium arrives as the cliffhanger and recognizes the blade. Lyra is preserved as the permanent researcher/mage and enters as the wider-world bridge rather than replacing Mira.
